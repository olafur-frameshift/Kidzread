KIDSREAD — MANIFESTO
====================

We are building something that matters.

Every child deserves to learn to read. Not as a chore, not under pressure,
not in fear of getting it wrong — but with joy, with wonder, and at their
own pace. KidsRead exists to make that first step into literacy feel like
play.

WHY THIS EXISTS
---------------
The gap between children who arrive at school as confident readers and those
who don't is one of the most consequential divides in a child's life. It
shapes everything that follows. Most reading apps treat this as a content
problem — more words, more levels, more gamification. We believe it is a
confidence problem. A child who is afraid to be wrong stops trying. A child
who is having fun keeps going.

WHAT WE STAND FOR
-----------------
No wrong answers, only next tries.
  Every mistake is met with encouragement, never shame. The app wiggles,
  smiles, and says "try again!" — not a buzzer, not a red X.

Audio first, reading second.
  A child learning to read cannot read instructions. Every prompt is spoken
  aloud. Navigation is driven by icons and sound. The app works for a child
  who cannot yet read a single word.

Small hands, big targets.
  Every touchable element is at least 60×60dp. No fiddly taps, no accidental
  back buttons. The interface is built for hands that are still learning motor
  control.

Short sessions, deep learning.
  Each level takes 2–3 minutes. A child's attention is precious. We respect
  it by packing maximum learning into minimum time, then letting them go play.

Rewards that mean something.
  Stars and unlockable games aren't hollow. They represent real progress —
  letters mastered, words recognised, sounds decoded. The child earns them;
  the child owns them.

Progress is personal.
  No leaderboards. No comparisons. No "you're behind." Just one child's
  journey, measured only against their own yesterday.

THE PROMISE
-----------
When a child finishes KidsRead, they can:
  - Name and recognise every letter of the alphabet
  - Match uppercase and lowercase pairs
  - Decode the sounds letters make
  - Spell simple three-letter words
  - Match spoken and written words to pictures

That is a foundation. Everything else — stories, books, a lifetime of
reading — is built on it.

We are not building an app. We are building the first step.

Build it with care. Build it with joy. Build it like a child is watching —
because one is.
